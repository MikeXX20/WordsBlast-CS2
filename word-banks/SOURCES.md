# Word Bank Sources

These word banks are curated for English comms practice, not copied as exhaustive map diagrams. Callouts can vary by region, team, update, and rank. The safest classroom workflow is to teach these common names first, then let players add team-specific names later.

## Suggested lesson flow

1. Start with `pro-fps-comms.txt`.
2. Teach one game at a time with either `cs2-map-callouts.txt` or `valorant-map-callouts.txt`.
3. Move into real conditions with `cs2-real-match-comms.txt` or `valorant-real-match-comms.txt`.
4. In the app, use word-to-meaning first.
5. Switch to meaning-to-word when learners can recognize the terms.
6. Ask players to say one complete sentence after each answer, such as `Two B main, spike down`.
