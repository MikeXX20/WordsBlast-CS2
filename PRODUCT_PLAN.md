# WordsBlast Product Plan

## Opinion

WordsBlast has a strong hook: vocabulary practice feels faster and more memorable when it borrows the energy of a game. The product should not try to compete with full flashcard platforms first. Its wedge is a focused, fun, low-friction trainer for students who already have vocabulary lists and want practice that feels less dull.

## Best Audience

- Chinese ESL learners and bilingual classrooms
- Students preparing for vocabulary-heavy exams
- Teachers who want a quick practice game for custom word lists
- Streamers or gaming communities that like CS2-inspired training themes

## Monetization

Start with a free local trainer and one lightweight paid offer:

- Free: custom import, bilingual UI, local progress, daily goal, weak-word export
- Founder Pro at $4/month: premium word packs, custom sound kits, detailed streak analytics, cloud backup, and classroom deck sharing
- Teacher Pack at $19/class/month later: shared decks, classroom leaderboard, exportable reports

## Next Revenue Experiments

1. Add a Stripe or Lemon Squeezy checkout for Founder Pro.
2. Ship three paid deck packs: TOEFL basics, IELTS academic, and CS2/gaming English.
3. Add an email waitlist form to measure demand before building backend accounts.
4. Publish a short demo video and link directly to the hosted app.
5. Track activation: imported deck, first game started, first review list exported, daily goal completed.

## Product Principles

- The game must stay usable before payment.
- Paid value should save time, preserve progress, or unlock high-quality content.
- Teachers need exports and class workflows more than cosmetic upgrades.
- The CS2 identity should be energetic, but the interface should remain clean enough for classrooms.
